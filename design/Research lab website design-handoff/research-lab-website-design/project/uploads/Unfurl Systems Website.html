Open invitation

Challenge the model with real domains.

The useful contribution is not vague agreement. It is a concrete component, device, workflow, policy, or enterprise capability that tests whether the DCP claim shape can describe reality without hiding risk.

Open an issue Contact the lab

Good contribution prompts

What does the component own, and where does that ownership end?
What should it explicitly refuse to own?
Which dependencies, ports, faults, and human escalation points are required?

About

A research-first surface, not a product launch page.

Unfurl Systems is being presented as a public research effort around capability contracts, governed assembly, and human review. The website should make the thinking approachable, the work inspectable, and the open questions visible.

Design guardrails applied

No invented blog posts. No fake maintainer names. No Flow or Foundry public Labs. Correct GitHub organization: UnfurlSystemsLab.
